# Passenger Baggage & Travel Policy

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes Northstar Air rules for passenger carry-on items, checked baggage, special items, travel with children and pets, and selected ticket-change and refund situations.

2. Carry-On Baggage and Personal Items
2.1 Standard Carry-On Allowance
Passengers traveling on Standard, Flex, or Premium fares may bring one carry-on bag and one personal item into the cabin, subject to space and safety restrictions. The carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. The personal item must fit completely under the seat in front of the passenger.
Example rule: A backpack may qualify as the personal item if it fits within the personal-item dimensions and can be stowed fully under the seat.

2.2 Personal Item Definition
A personal item may include a backpack, purse, briefcase, laptop bag, or similarly sized item. Maximum personal-item dimensions are 18 x 14 x 8 inches, including handles and wheels. A backpack that exceeds these dimensions is treated as a carry-on bag rather than a personal item.

2.3 Basic Saver Fare Exception
Basic Saver passengers are limited to one personal item and do not receive a standard carry-on allowance. Full-size carry-on permitted only with eligible status.

6. Pets in the Cabin
An in-cabin pet carrier counts as the passenger’s personal item. A passenger traveling with a pet may still bring one carry-on bag if the fare otherwise includes a carry-on allowance.
